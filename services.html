Four services that keep a site moving

Take one of them or all four. The commercial terms stay flexible; the standard of the people does not.

Service 01

Manpower outsourcing

Whether you want manpower for a day or a year, we provide a team or the individuals who match the requirement. Workers remain on our sponsorship, so a closed visa quota never delays your start.

What the rate includes

  • Skilled, semi-skilled and general workers
  • Trade tested and certified before deployment
  • Sponsorship, visas and legal compliance held by us
  • Accommodation, transport and payroll managed
  • Short-term cover or long-term contracts
Service 02

Construction

Civil crews with their own chargehands, foremen and supervisors, so the trade arrives as a working unit rather than a collection of individuals you have to organise.

Trades on the ground

  • Steel fixing, shuttering and formwork
  • Block, plaster, tiling and interlock masonry
  • Carpentry: finishing, gypsum and furniture
  • Painting and surface preparation
  • Site supervision and safety oversight
Service 03

Facility management

Buildings run better when the same team sees them every day. We staff and manage the operation, then report on it monthly against agreed standards.

How the contract runs

  • Hard and soft services staffing
  • Housekeeping, security and reception cover
  • Technicians on planned rotation
  • Consumables and asset tracking
  • Monthly reporting against agreed standards
Service 04

Maintenance

We deliver first-class maintenance in shopping malls, offices, public and residential buildings, factories, stadiums, parks, hospitals, airports and more. Our division carries out the following activities.

01

All electrical, plumbing, drainage and A/C works.

02

Small civil works: rebuilding walls, doors and windows.

03

Dismantling ceramics and marble, then supply and fix in rooms, bathrooms, halls and kitchens.

04

Painting of all types after removing the existing coat by water jet or other methods.

05

Changing and maintaining doors and joinery, complete with painting.

06

Supply and fix of false ceilings, all systems.
